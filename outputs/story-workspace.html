“Hold still,” Maya said. “You’re bleeding on my floor.”

Damian had a complaint ready. It disappeared when she leaned closer, her brow drawn in concentration.

“What?” she demanded. “Is there something on my face?”

“No.” He paused. “I just like it.”

Her fingers stopped.

“Idiot,” she muttered.
